# **App Name**: Veridian IQ

## Core Features:

- Neural Crop Diagnosis: AI-powered computer vision system to identify crop diseases, leaf infections, and pest damage from photo uploads or real-time camera feeds.
- Veridian Assistant Tool: A generative AI chatbot that acts as a tool to reason through soil data, weather conditions, and plant health to provide multi-lingual farming advice.
- Agro-Predictive Engine: Utilizes machine learning models to provide yield predictions, harvest quantity estimates, and future market price forecasting for specific crops.
- Holographic Analytics Dashboard: A high-performance dark-themed UI featuring interactive charts for live weather, soil moisture (IoT simulation), and harvest trends.
- Agri-Nexus Marketplace: A central marketplace and community hub for equipment rental, seed purchasing, and knowledge sharing between farming organizations.
- Autonomous Irrigation Recommender: An intelligent logic system that analyzes rainfall and soil pH data stored in the database to generate smart water usage schedules.
- Auto-Generated Field Reports: Instant generation of downloadable PDF health reports using the results of AI diagnoses and real-time environmental data.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Vibrant Neon Green (#00FF66) to symbolize futuristic biological growth and energy.
- Background color: Deep Dark Charcoal (#0D110F) with a hint of forest green to ground the futuristic aesthetic.
- Accent color: Bright Electric Lime (#CFFF00) for highlighting high-importance call-to-actions and status indicators.
- Font pairing: 'Space Grotesk' for futuristic headlines and 'Inter' for highly legible body text and data tables.
- Fine-lined neon-glowing icons representing biology, hardware sensors, and cloud systems, using a consistent weight.
- A dashboard-centric layout featuring dark glassmorphism effects, floating glowing cards, and bento-box grid patterns for data visualization.
- Smooth micro-interactions such as scanlines for camera detection and subtle pulsating glows for live alerts.
